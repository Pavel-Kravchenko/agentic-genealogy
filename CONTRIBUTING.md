# Contributing

What helps most:

- a dated correction to a site's behaviour (it moved, closed, changed its block page, added an index);
- a guide for a region or archive that is not covered, in the same shape as the existing ones;
- a trap you paid for, as a row for `docs/errors-and-solutions.md`: symptom, cause, fix.

Rules:

1. **No real people.** Not in examples, not in issue text, not in screenshots. Use the invented cast of
   this repository (Primerov, Obraztsov, Näidis, Paraugs; the village of Primerovka) and placeholders for
   shelf marks and record ids. Do not post your own family's names, villages or documents.
2. **Polite access only.** Describe what a site holds, what a visitor sees on its public pages, how it
   signals a refusal, and what needs the researcher's own login in their own browser. Contributions that
   describe undocumented endpoints, request rates or ways around captchas, firewalls, bans or paywalls are
   not accepted.
3. **Observations carry a month and year.** A site that did not answer from your network is reported as
   that, with the official alternative (a written request, a visit).
4. **Nothing from memory.** Give the URL of the site or the title of the published guide you rely on.
5. Tools: offline only, no network clients; Python 3.9 syntax, standard library where possible, a zero
   that says how reliable it is, no silent fallbacks.
6. **Neutral wording about sites and institutions.** Write what you saw and when, not a verdict. Do not
   point to copies of books or databases whose rights status you do not know.

By contributing you agree that your contribution is published under the licence of the part you change
(MIT for `tools/` and `examples/`, CC BY 4.0 for the rest).

Issues and pull requests that contain personal data of real people are edited or removed. Institutions and
persons who want a statement corrected or removed: see "Corrections and removal" in the
[README](README.md#good-to-know).
